## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-fidelity sustainability-tech aesthetic engineered to project rigorous operational competence, technological intelligence, and environmental stewardship. It departs from cliché earth-tone tropes in favor of an authoritative, clean, and forward-looking UI reminiscent of precision climate instruments and advanced enterprise software.

### Tone & Personality
- **Rigorous & Trustworthy:** Authoritative visual cues evoke precision data science, machine learning models, and validated ecological metrics.
- **Optimistic & Clean:** Ample spatial breathing room, light-reflective canvas tones, and sharp contrast eliminate visual noise, rendering complex sorting decisions intuitive.
- **Engineered Naturalism:** Natural greens are deployed as intentional operational signifiers, pairing deep forest depths with high-efficiency energy accents.

### Visual Style
A tailored blend of **Minimalism** and **Tactile Modernism**:
- Controlled, border-led architecture with crisp structural separation.
- Micro-elevations utilizing soft, natural ambient shadows rather than harsh layered drops.
- Pill geometries, dynamic telemetry meters, and balanced structural radii that mirror circular resource flows without sacrificing data density.

## Colors

The palette is tuned around high-legibility green spectral tiers against an organic-tinted light canvas, maintaining strict WCAG AAA conformance for core typography and AA for interactive states.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#14532D` - Forest Deep):** Anchors high-priority structural frames, navigation bars, primary CTAs, and foundational headers. Evokes longevity, ecological permanence, and institutional security.
- **Secondary (`#16A34A` - Vivid Emerald):** The primary interaction and affirmative feedback signal. Applied to active states, validation marks, confirmed diversion statuses, and interactive switches.
- **Tertiary (`#A3E635` - Electric Lime):** The system's technological spark. Reserved exclusively for high-signal highlights, real-time AI recognition badges, circular progress loops, and telemetry tags. Never used for body text.
- **Canvas Base (`#F7FAF5` - Alabaster Moss):** A low-luminance tinted off-white that prevents screen glare and sets a warm, sustainable foundation.
- **Neutral Foreground (`#0F172A` - Dark Slate):** High-contrast neutral for primary typographic content and dense numerical readouts.
- **Neutral Muted (`#475569` - Slate Green Mid):** Form labels, secondary meta-information, inactive tab indicators, and contextual guidance.
- **Surface Stroke (`#E2E8F0` / `#DCFCE7` mix):** Calibrated border tinting to define card containment without stark boundaries.

## Typography

The typography system relies on a functional trio to separate editorial authority, scanning legibility, and technical accuracy:

1. **Display & Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its friendly yet sculpted geometric curves. It grounds the platform's vision with contemporary polish and warmth.
2. **Body (Manrope):** Delivers superior legibility across varied device viewports. Balanced x-heights and open counters ensure high comprehension when reading sorting criteria, environmental reports, and platform guidance.
3. **Labels & AI Telemetry (Space Grotesk):** Deployed for metrics, confidence percentages, bin designations, and hardware sensor data. Its monospaced-adjacent character creates a direct link to algorithmic classification and precision engineering.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model leverages a dynamic 12-column fluid grid system on desktop that gracefully collapses to an 8-column layout on tablet, and a 4-column layout on mobile viewports.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Bounds
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4 columns, 16px margins, 16px gutters. Single-stack module arrangement optimized for one-hand thumb interaction. Bottom sheets and thumb-driven scanning interactions dominate.
- **Tablet (640px – 1024px):** 8 columns, 32px margins, 20px gutters. Dual-pane viewports pairing live classification inputs with telemetry streams.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns, max bounded width of 1440px with dynamic outer margins (minimum 48px). Enables persistent lateral navigation and multi-stream bin telemetry.

### Spacing Principles
- Density follows function: analytical metrics and dashboard cards employ compact interior paddings (`space-md` to `space-lg`), while public discovery and onboarding views employ expansive visual rhythm (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`).
- Grid components conform to standard 8-point base intervals, with micro-spacing anchored to 4-point subdivisions for tight badge-to-icon locks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects excessive, theatrical drop shadows in favor of ambient dispersion tinted with subtle environmental pigmentation. Surfaces feel physical, lightweight, and clean.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Floor Canvas):** Flat `#F7FAF5` background with zero displacement.
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Modules):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface bordered by a crisp `1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`. Elevated by an ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.03), 0 6px 16px -4px rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered States & Active Modals):** Slight vertical lift. Shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.05), 0 12px 24px -6px rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.08)`. Border subtly shifts to `rgba(22, 163, 74, 0.2)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Action Bars & Live AI Detection Overlays):** Semi-translucent white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`) supported by a heavy backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`). Shadow: `box-shadow: 0 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.

### Dividers & Structural Separation
Dividers must remain discreet: hairline 1px rules painted in `#E2E8F0` or `#DCFCE7` (when residing within active green modules).

## Shapes

The shape architecture reflects natural cycles, softness, and modern industrial industrial design:

- **Base Controls & Inputs (`rounded-xl` / 16px):** Yields comfortable tap targets for fields, controls, and compact buttons.
- **Surface Cards & Enclosures (`rounded-2xl` / 24px to `rounded-3xl` / 32px):** Larger containers utilize sweeping, gentle corners that present data density with calm approachability.
- **Tags, System Badges, and Micro-Triggers (`rounded-full` / Pill-shape):** Status labels, waste categories (e.g., *Compostable*, *Recyclable*), and filter toggles are strictly pill-shaped. This geometry directly references circular economy motifs and organic, pebble-like smoothness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep forest green background (`#14532D`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), `font-family: Plus Jakarta Sans`, semibold weight, rounded pill (`rounded-full`), padded with `12px 24px`. Hover initiates a transition to a deep leaf tone (`#166534`) with an ambient shadow burst.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent base enclosed by a 1.5px border in `#16A34A`, emerald text (`#16A34A`). Hover transitions background to `#DCFCE7` at 50% opacity.
- **Tertiary Accent (Action Trigger):** Lime fill (`#A3E635`) with dark slate text (`#0F172A`) for primary in-flow triggers like "Confirm Scan" or "Empty Bin".

### Pill Tags & Badges
- Constructed with `label-pill` typography in Space Grotesk, all-caps, with 0.06em tracking.
- Category indicators use subtle tinted backgrounds:
  - *Organic / Compostable:* Deep forest tint `#DCFCE7` with `#14532D` text.
  - *Plastics / Metals:* Slate-cyan tint `#E0F2FE` with `#0369A1` text.
  - *Hazardous / E-Waste:* Amber-sand tint `#FEF3C7` with `#B45309` text.
- Integrated with mini Lucide-style monoline icons (14px) vertically centered on the left.

### Cards & Modules
- Double-tier card rounding: Standard metric cards employ `rounded-2xl` (24px); major contextual hero modules employ `rounded-3xl` (32px).
- Internal content margins use `space-lg` (24px) to preserve clarity.
- Interactive cards feature a micro-scale upward translation (-2px) and subtle emerald border reveal on hover.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Input Fields:** `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#CBD5E1` border, 16px border-radius, `14px 18px` internal padding. Focus transitions to a 1.5px border of `#16A34A` accompanied by an emerald focus ring (`rgba(22, 163, 74, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Fully rounded controls (`rounded-md` for checkboxes, `rounded-full` for radios). Unchecked: `#F8FAFC` base with `#94A3B8` border. Checked: `#16A34A` fill with crisp white internal iconography.

### Specialized AI Telemetry & Progress
- **AI Material Confidence Bar:** Thin, 6px track in `#E2E8F0` populated by an animated progress vector in `#16A34A` terminating in an `#A3E635` micro-indicator. Accompanying probability reads in Space Grotesk.
- **Circular Diversion Ring:** Scalable circular SVG meters displaying the diversion rate percentage centered in bold Plus Jakarta Sans, utilizing an energetic gradient running from `#14532D` to `#A3E635`.
